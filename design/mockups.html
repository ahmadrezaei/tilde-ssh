The SSH key bar: pages, modifiers, gestures

Pinned esc ctrl alt on the left and the arrow joystick on the right never move. The six keys in the middle page horizontally. Small grey glyphs are flick-up alternates. Every key is customizable, with presets for DevOps, Vim, tmux, Emacs, Kubernetes and Minimal.

Default "DevOps" profile: 5 pages

One row, 46 pt. Each key is 36×38 pt on a 402 pt-wide iPhone.

Arrow joystick ✣

Drag from ✣ in any direction. Past 8 pt it sends that arrow, and the further you drag the faster it repeats (8 / 20 / 40 Hz). Each step gives a haptic tick. Tap swaps the middle keys for ← ↓ ↑ → home end. It is DECCKM-aware (\eOA vs \e[A).

iPad + hardware keyboard

The system keyboard is hidden, so the bar collapses to a 32 pt strip (or hides; it's a setting). Option = Meta, ⌘. = Esc, all app shortcuts are ⌘-only so Ctrl always reaches the shell.
