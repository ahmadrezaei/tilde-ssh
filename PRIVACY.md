# Privacy Policy

**Tilde – SSH Client & Terminal**
Developer: Ahmad Rezaei
Effective date: 2026-11-26

## The short version

Tilde collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no third-party SDKs. I run no servers for Tilde and receive nothing from your use of it.

## What stays on your devices and in your iCloud

- **Your server list.** Hosts, groups, snippets and settings sync between your devices through your own private iCloud storage (the CloudKit private database). That data belongs to your Apple Account. I cannot see or read it.
- **Passwords and private keys.** These are stored in your iCloud Keychain. Apple handles storage and syncing, and they are end-to-end encrypted. I cannot read them.
- **Known host fingerprints.** These are stored with your server data, in the same private iCloud storage.

## Connections

When you connect to a server, the connection goes directly from your device to that server over SSH. It does not pass through anything I operate. What you do on your servers is between you and them.

## Face ID and Touch ID

If you turn on app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is handled entirely by your device's operating system. Tilde only learns whether authentication succeeded. It never sees or stores biometric data.

## Local network access

Tilde asks for local network access only so it can reach your own hosts on your home or office network. It does not scan the network or collect information about other devices.

## Children

Tilde does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this file in the Tilde repository, with a new effective date. The history is visible in the repository's commit log.
